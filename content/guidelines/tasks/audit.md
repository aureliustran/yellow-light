# Task: audit a range of chapters

**Trigger:** "rà soát ch.N–M", or before a new phase.

## Steps
1. Use the skill `den-vang-ra-soat` (parallel readers, canon ledger, character arcs, threads, clues, motifs, style; independent evaluation).
2. Write `audits/AUD-NNN/` (until migrated: the folder the skill uses).
3. Every contradiction becomes a `Q-` or a fix in the list; decisions the author takes go through `record-decision`.

## Checks
Every contradiction has a `Q-` or a fix item; blueprints of the range synced or flagged.
